Firm U and Firm L Question c

Return on equity (ROE) is net income divided by book equity. Times interest earned (TIE) is operating earnings divided by interest. Firm U has no interest expense, so its TIE is undefined.

CDSS buybacks Question d

Earnings per share (EPS) use the shares remaining after the buyback. The weighted average cost of capital (WACC) combines the after-tax cost of debt with the cost of equity.
